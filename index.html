Pilot protocol
  1. Before engine start, open the app and complete Preflight: date, pilot, aircraft, Hobbs start, flight type.
  2. Predator control: enter every hunter aboard and their license or permit number before takeoff.
  3. During the flight, tap the counters as animals are taken or counted. The app saves as you go.
  4. After shutdown, enter Hobbs end and write up the paper log as usual.
  5. Photograph the log page in the app. The flight cannot be submitted without it.
  6. Review and submit before leaving the aircraft. No signal is fine: the flight is held on the phone and sends itself once you're back in range.
